What this place is

A place where you feel truly heard and understood. The therapist is there only for you, open to whatever you are willing to bring. There is no hidden interest, no game, no agenda. You can say all your thoughts out loud and they will be received with gentleness and kindness.

Sometimes you discover what it is like to be in a relationship with an authentic person. And that experience, in itself, helps.

Who comes here

People who prefer to talk about what matters to them in English. Some have moved to Bucharest for work or for a partner. Some are Romanians who have lived abroad, or who simply find it easier to say certain things in English.

  • Something isn't working and you can't name what.
  • Anxiety, pressure at work, nights when your mind doesn't stop.
  • Your relationship is under strain, or you keep ending up in the same kind of relationship.
  • You are going through a separation, possibly far from the people who would normally support you.

You don't need a diagnosis to come. You don't need to know exactly what to say.

Individual therapy

Sessions last 50 minutes, at the practice or online, by video. Most people come weekly at the start. We decide the rhythm together.

What often happens: people are surprised by their own words. They come close to a feeling they've been avoiding, try a few times, and at some point let themselves feel it. What they find is not terrible. It is what it is. And once it is known, it becomes bearable, and you can start looking for solutions.

Couples therapy

You can come together, or one of you can come alone first. Couples sessions last 80 minutes. We work on what is happening between you now: the arguments that repeat, the silence, the distance. The aim is not to keep you together at any cost. It is that whatever you decide, you decide it clearly.

My approach

I work in Person-Centred Therapy (Carl Rogers), Motivational Interviewing and Clinical Hypnosis. In plain terms: the relationship between us is the main tool. I don't diagnose you from the outside. We look at what you bring, at your pace, and find solutions together.

About me

I'm Edi Vlaston, psychologist and psychotherapist, registered with the Romanian College of Psychologists (code 22334). Before becoming a therapist I spent more than 20 years leading and guiding people under pressure, most of it in international, English-speaking teams.

Questions

Are sessions fully in English?

Yes. The whole session is in English, from the first minute to the last, at the practice or online.

How do I book?

Pick a slot in the calendar. The booking page is in English and you get a confirmation by email. If none of the times work for you, write to me on WhatsApp.

Is it confidential?

Yes. What you say in session stays there, under the professional code of the Romanian College of Psychologists.

Can we come as a couple if only one of us lives in Bucharest?

Yes. Sessions can be held online, so you can join from different places.

What if I'm not sure therapy is for me?

Book one session. You'll know more after it than before.
